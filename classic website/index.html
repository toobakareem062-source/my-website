About us

Classic Enterprise

Classic Enterprise is a leading sourcing company connecting leading international manufacturers with the pharmaceutical industry.

Over the years, we have established a strong network with leading pharmaceutical and consumer healthcare companies across Pakistan and the Middle East, including major multinational corporations and highly reputable regional businesses. We provide our customers with reliable and high-quality solutions for all their sourcing needs. Our core expertise includes pharmaceutical packaging materials such as ALU-ALU / cold-form foil, aluminium blister and strip foil, PVC blister film, and PVDC-coated PVC, alongside a growing portfolio of APIs, excipients, intermediates, and other pharmaceutical solutions.

At Classic Enterprise, our focus is on reliability, transparency, and long-term partnerships with all our partners.
